description: Three-ingredient banana pancakes.
category: Breakfast
time: 15 min
servings: 2
difficulty: 1
ingredients:
  - name: Ripe banana
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Egg
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Vanilla extract
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Oat flour
    amount: 100
    unit: "ml"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mash the banana and mix in the egg and vanilla.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Stir in the oat flour until combined.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Fry small pancakes in a pan until set and golden.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Top with whatever you like.</p>
</div>
