<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Preheat the oven to <strong>180°C</strong>. Pour the milk over the bread and leave to soak for 10 minutes.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Heat the butter in a heavy-bottomed pot over moderate heat. Add the oil and brown the onions. Stir in the curry powder, sugar, salt and pepper and cook for 2 minutes. Add the meat; when it changes colour, add the lemon juice and bring to the boil.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Squeeze the bread completely dry, <strong>reserving the milk</strong>. Add the bread, one egg, the apple, raisins and almonds to the meat. Mix well until everything binds. Taste and adjust salt.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Spoon into a greased baking dish and press the lemon or bay leaves into the surface. Beat the remaining 2 eggs with the reserved milk until foamy and pour evenly over the meat.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bake on the middle rack for <strong>30 minutes</strong>, until browned and firm.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Geelrys:</strong> melt the butter in a large pot, stir in the rice to coat, then add the water, cinnamon, turmeric, saffron, salt and sugar. Bring to the boil, lower the heat, sprinkle the raisins on top, cover and steam for about <strong>20 minutes</strong> until plump. Serve alongside the bobotie.</p>
</div>
