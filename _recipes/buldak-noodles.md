---
layout: recipe
title: Kerusha's Buldak Noodles
image: /assets/images/buldak-noodles.svg
description: Flexible Korean-inspired noodles built around Buldak instant noodles — pick your heat, add any protein and veg you have.
category: Mains
tags: [Warm]
time: 30 min
servings: 2
difficulty: 1
ingredients:
  - name: Buldak noodle packets (black = super hot, red = hot, pink = milder, yellow = cheesier)
    amount: 2
    unit: "packets"
  - name: Sauce sachet (from the packet)
    amount: 0
    unit: ""
  - name: Sprinkle sachet (from the packet, for garnish)
    amount: 0
    unit: ""
  - name: Onion, chopped
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Garlic, chopped
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Tomato, chopped (or tomato paste)
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: BBQ spice or mixed herbs (nothing hot — the sauce brings the heat)
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Protein — prawns, chicken strips or beef mince
    amount: 0
    unit: "your choice"
  - name: Vegetables — peppers, mushrooms, carrots or coleslaw mix
    amount: 0
    unit: "any combination"
  - name: Beef stock or soup
    amount: 0
    unit: "optional, to thicken"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Build the base.</strong> Fry the chopped onion and garlic until brown, then add the chopped tomato or tomato paste and stir through.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add your protein and season with BBQ spice, mixed herbs, or whatever you prefer — but <strong>nothing hot</strong>; the Buldak sauce brings all the heat.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">While the base cooks, soak the noodles in hot water for about <strong>5 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the veggies to the pan with a splash of water if needed, and cook for another 10 minutes.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Drain the noodles and add them to the pan along with the sauce sachet. Mix everything through, lower the heat and let it slow cook so the flavours combine. Optional: stir in beef stock or soup to thicken.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Garnish with the sprinkle sachet and serve.</p>
</div>
