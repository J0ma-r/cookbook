<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Marinate.</strong> Toss the chicken with salt, pepper, 1 tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp turmeric and the lemon juice. Set aside for about 30 minutes while you prepare the rest. (Optional: add a sprinkle of chicken or Cajun spice.)</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Sear.</strong> Melt 2 tbsp butter in a saucepan and fry the marinated chicken until browned. Remove and set aside.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Build the sauce.</strong> In the same pan, melt another 2 tbsp butter and fry the onion until caramelised. Add the garam masala, chilli powder, cayenne, cumin, turmeric, ginger-garlic paste and cinnamon sticks; fry briefly to release the flavours.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the tomato purée and about 1 cup of water (more for a thinner sauce). Lower the heat, stir in the cream, and slowly bring back up until bubbling. Salt to taste.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Remove the cinnamon sticks and <strong>blend the sauce until smooth</strong>. Return it to the pan with the chicken and simmer for 10&ndash;15 minutes.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Stir in the remaining 2 tbsp butter and simmer a few minutes more until rich and glossy. Serve with basmati rice or naan.</p>
</div>
