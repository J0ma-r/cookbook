---
layout: recipe
title: Butternut & Chicken Pastry
image: /assets/images/butternut-chicken-pastry.jpg
description: Puff pastry tart with roast butternut, chicken and mozzarella.
category: Chicken
time: 50 min
servings: 4
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Butternut, diced
    amount: 400
    unit: "g"
  - name: Puff pastry
    amount: 1
    unit: "sheet"
  - name: Chicken, diced
    amount: 300
    unit: "g"
  - name: Mozzarella
    amount: 125
    unit: "g"
  - name: Fresh basil
    amount: 0
    unit: "a handful"
  - name: Salt & pepper
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Roast the diced butternut for <strong>20 minutes</strong> until tender.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Par-fry the chicken until lightly coloured.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Roll out the puff pastry and top with the butternut, chicken, mozzarella, basil and spices.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bake for <strong>25 minutes at 180&deg;C</strong> until the pastry is golden and puffed.</p>
</div>
