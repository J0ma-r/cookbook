---
layout: recipe
title: Chicken Alfredo
image: /assets/images/chicken-alfredo.jpg
description: Creamy fettuccine with seared chicken and parmesan.
category: Pasta
time: 30 min
servings: 4
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Chicken breast
    amount: 2
    unit: pieces
  - name: Fettuccine
    amount: 320
    unit: g
  - name: Heavy cream
    amount: 200
    unit: ml
  - name: Parmesan, grated
    amount: 60
    unit: g
  - name: Garlic clove, minced
    amount: 2
    unit: ""
  - name: Butter
    amount: 30
    unit: g
  - name: Salt & pepper
    amount: 0
    unit: to taste
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Season the chicken with <strong>salt and pepper</strong>, then sear in butter over medium-high heat until golden and cooked through, about 6 minutes per side. Rest, then slice.</p>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Cook the fettuccine in salted water until <strong>al dente</strong>. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.</p>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">In the same pan, soften the garlic in butter, pour in the cream, and simmer gently for 2 minutes. Stir in the parmesan until smooth.</p>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Toss the pasta in the sauce, loosening with pasta water as needed. Top with the sliced chicken and extra parmesan.</p>
</div>
