## Ingredients
- Chicken breast
- Pasta
- Cream
- Garlic
- Parmesan

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta
2. Cook chicken
3. Add cream and garlic
4. Mix everything together
5. Top with parmesan

## Notes
- Add mushrooms if you like
