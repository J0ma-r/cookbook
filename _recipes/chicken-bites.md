---
layout: recipe
title: Chicken Bites
image: /assets/images/chicken-bites.jpg
description: Sticky teriyaki chicken with onion soup and sesame.
category: Mains
time: 35 min
servings: 3
difficulty: 1
ingredients:
  - name: Chicken, diced
    amount: 550
    unit: "g"
  - name: Brown onion soup (dry)
    amount: 1
    unit: "packet"
  - name: Teriyaki sauce
    amount: 4
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Sesame seeds
    amount: 2
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Onion
    amount: 1
    unit: "large"
  - name: Water
    amount: 100
    unit: "ml"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Fry the onion until soft.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the diced chicken and par-fry until the outside is sealed.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Stir together the onion soup, teriyaki and water, then pour over the chicken.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bake for <strong>15 minutes at 180&deg;C</strong>. Toast the sesame seeds and scatter over before serving.</p>
</div>
