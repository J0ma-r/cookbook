  - name: Italian seasoning
    amount: 1

  - name: Kosher salt
    amount: 0

  - name: Black pepper
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Extra virgin olive oil
    amount: 2
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Penne pasta, preferably ridged
    amount: 225
    unit: "g"
  - name: Heavy cream

    unit: "cup"
  - name: Parmesan cheese, plus more to serve

  - name: Sun-dried tomatoes, drained

  - name: Basil pesto

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Toss the chicken pieces with the Italian seasoning and a generous pinch of salt and pepper.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bring a medium pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the penne until al dente, about <strong>8&ndash;9 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken, tossing occasionally, until golden and cooked through, about <strong>7&ndash;8 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Reserve about 1 cup of the pasta cooking water, then drain the pasta.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Lower the heat under the chicken and stir in the cooked pasta, cream, parmesan and sun-dried tomatoes. If the sauce looks dry, add reserved pasta water a couple of tablespoons at a time until glossy — stirring vigorously helps the starch thicken the sauce.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Take the pan off the heat and stir in the pesto. Finish with grated parmesan, red pepper flakes and torn basil. Serve immediately.</p>
</div>
