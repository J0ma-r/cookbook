<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bring a pot of well-salted water to the boil, add the chicken breasts and boil for <strong>30 minutes</strong> until cooked through.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Lift the chicken out and let it cool slightly. Remove the skin (and any bones), then <strong>pull the meat apart with two forks</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Meanwhile, make the pesto: blend the roasted pumpkin seeds, parmesan, basil, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil and a pinch of salt until it reaches your desired texture &mdash; chunky or smooth.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mix the pulled chicken with the pesto until well coated. Taste and adjust the salt.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Lay out the wraps, add lettuce and sliced avocado, spoon over the <strong>chicken pesto</strong> and roll up tightly. Slice in half to serve.</p>
</div>
