---
layout: recipe
title: Chickpea Curry
image: /assets/images/chickpea-curry.jpg
description: A quick coconut chickpea curry with greens.
category: Vegetarian
time: 30 min
servings: 4
difficulty: 1
ingredients:
  - name: Chickpeas
    amount: 400
    unit: "g"
  - name: Chopped tomatoes
    amount: 400
    unit: "g"
  - name: Coconut milk
    amount: 400
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Purple onion, diced
    amount: 1
    unit: "medium"
  - name: Curry powder
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Cumin
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Paprika
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Chilli
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Kale, spinach or basil
    amount: 0
    unit: "optional"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Soften the diced purple onion in a pan with a little oil.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Stir in the curry powder, cumin, paprika and chilli and cook until fragrant.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the chickpeas, tomatoes and coconut milk.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Simmer until thickened. Stir through the greens at the end if using.</p>
</div>
