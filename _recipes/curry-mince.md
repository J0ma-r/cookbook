---

title: Renette's Ouma's Curry Mince
image: /assets/images/curry-mince.svg
description: A family-favourite South African curry mince — rich, mildly spiced and warming. Best served with homemade vetkoek.
category: Mains
tags: [Warm]
time: 30 min
servings: 4
difficulty: 1
ingredients:
  - name: Large onion, chopped
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Rajah curry powder
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Mince
    amount: 400
    unit: "g"
  - name: Beef stock
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Tomato paste
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Canned curry mixed veg
    amount: 1
    unit: "can"
  - name: Canned chopped and peeled tomatoes
    amount: 1
    unit: "can"
  - name: Chutney
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Salt & pepper
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Olive oil
    amount: 0
    unit: "for frying"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Brown the onion in olive oil in a pot.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the Rajah curry powder and cook for about <strong>2 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the mince and brown it, along with the half cup of beef stock.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the tomato paste and mix everything together until the mince is almost cooked.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add both cans (curry mixed veg and chopped tomatoes) and cook for about <strong>5 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Stir in the chutney, salt and pepper to taste. Best served with homemade vetkoek.</p>
</div>
