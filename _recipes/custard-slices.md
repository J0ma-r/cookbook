  - name: Cream Cracker biscuits

    amount: 200

    amount: 50

    unit: "g"

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Prepare the dish.</strong> Grease a glass dish (35 x 25 x 5 cm). Pack 15 cream crackers into the base in rows of 5 x 3, with all the halving lines running in the same direction.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Make the custard.</strong> Bring the milk to the boil in a large pot, add the sugar and butter and stir until melted. Remove from the heat.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">In a separate bowl, mix the cake flour, cornflour, custard powder, salt and cold water thoroughly. Add the egg yolks and beat well. Gradually whisk the milk mixture in, then return everything to the pot and stir constantly over medium heat for about <strong>5 minutes</strong>, until thick.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Off the heat, stir in the vanilla. Beat the egg whites to soft peaks and gently fold them into the custard.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Assemble.</strong> Pour the warm custard over the biscuits, then cover with another 15 biscuits in the same 5 x 3 pattern, lines matching the bottom layer. Cool, then refrigerate until completely cold — <strong>ideally overnight</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Ice and serve.</strong> Mix the icing sugar and boiling water until smooth, pour slowly over the biscuits and spread evenly. Refrigerate until set, then cut slices the size of half a cream cracker.</p>
</div>
