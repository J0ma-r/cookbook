---
layout: recipe
title: Dosa
image: /assets/images/dosa.jpg
description: A savoury Indian-style pancake with a spiced potato filling.
category: Mains
vegetarian: true
time: 45 min
servings: 3
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Cumin
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Onion, chopped
    amount: 1
    unit: "medium"
  - name: Pre-boiled potato
    amount: 2
    unit: "medium"
  - name: Garlic, diced
    amount: 2
    unit: "cloves"
  - name: Ginger, diced
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Chilli, seedless
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Flour
    amount: 100
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Milk
    amount: 200
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Egg
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Mustard seeds
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Turmeric
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Toast the cumin with a little salt.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the diced ginger, garlic and chilli, cook briefly, then set aside in a bowl.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Fry the chopped onion with the mustard seeds and turmeric.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the pre-boiled potato and fry until cooked through.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Make a batter with the milk, egg, flour and the reserved chilli-garlic-ginger mix.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Cook thin pancakes from the batter and fill with the potato mix.</p>
</div>
