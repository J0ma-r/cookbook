<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Cook the rice (or use leftover rice).</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Beat the eggs and scramble them in a hot wok.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the rice and soy sauce and stir-fry.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Toss through the spring onions and any extras like chicken, pork or veg.</p>
</div>
