time: 1 hr 30 min
servings: 3
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Unsalted butter
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Extra virgin olive oil
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Yellow onions, thinly sliced
    amount: 3
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Kosher salt
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Garlic cloves, minced
    amount: 3
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Orzo pasta, uncooked
    amount: 1
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Dry white wine
    amount: 0.25
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Sherry
    amount: 0.125
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Beef stock (unsalted recommended)
    amount: 2
    unit: "cups"
  - name: Black pepper
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Fresh thyme sprigs
    amount: 3
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Worcestershire sauce
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Half-and-half or heavy cream (optional)
    amount: 0.25

    amount: 115
    unit: "g"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Caramelise the onions.</strong> Melt the butter and olive oil in a large high-rimmed sauté pan over medium heat. Add the onions and a generous pinch of salt, stir, cover and cook for 5 minutes.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Remove the lid, lower the heat to medium-low and cook for <strong>30&ndash;60 minutes</strong>, stirring occasionally, until deeply caramelised. Add a splash of water if the pan starts to scorch.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Toast and deglaze.</strong> Add the garlic and uncooked orzo and toast for 1&ndash;2 minutes. Pour in the white wine and sherry, scraping up any stuck bits, and cook about 2 minutes until absorbed.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Simmer.</strong> Pour in the beef stock, black pepper, thyme leaves and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer, stirring often so the orzo doesn't stick. Add more stock if it absorbs too quickly — the orzo takes about <strong>15 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Finish.</strong> Lower the heat, stir in the half-and-half (if using), then the grated Gruyère until melted and combined. Serve immediately with extra black pepper, fresh thyme and more Gruyère.</p>
</div>
