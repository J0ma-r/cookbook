---
layout: recipe
title: Gnocchi
image: /assets/images/gnocchi.jpg
description: Homemade potato gnocchi pan-fried with mushrooms.
category: Pasta
time: 1 hr
servings: 3
difficulty: 3
ingredients:
  - name: Large potatoes
    amount: 2
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Flour
    amount: 1
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Ricotta or cream cheese
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Egg
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Mushrooms, diced
    amount: 1
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Salt & pepper
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Cream or hard cheese
    amount: 0
    unit: "optional"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Boil the potatoes until soft, then mash and cool slightly.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mix the potato with the flour, ricotta, egg and seasoning into a dough.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Roll into small balls and press the centre of each to flatten slightly.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Drop into boiling water and cook until they float, then drain.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Fry in a pan with the mushrooms. Finish with cream or grated hard cheese if you like.</p>
</div>
