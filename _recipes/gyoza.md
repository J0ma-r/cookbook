---
layout: recipe
title: Gyoza
image: /assets/images/gyoza.jpg
description: Pan-fried Japanese pork and cabbage dumplings, crisp on the bottom and steamed on top.
category: Dumplings
time: 1 hr
servings: 4
difficulty: 3
ingredients:
  - name: Gyoza wrappers
    amount: 30
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Pork mince
    amount: 250
    unit: "g"
  - name: Napa cabbage, finely chopped
    amount: 200
    unit: "g"
  - name: Garlic chives or spring onion
    amount: 4
    unit: "stalks"
  - name: Garlic, minced
    amount: 2
    unit: "cloves"
  - name: Ginger, grated
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Soy sauce
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Sesame oil
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Sugar
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Salt
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Cooking oil
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Water (for steaming)
    amount: 60
    unit: "ml"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Toss the chopped cabbage with the salt and leave for 10 minutes, then squeeze out as much liquid as you can.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mix the cabbage with the pork, chives, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil and sugar until well combined.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Place a teaspoon of filling in the centre of each wrapper. Wet the edge, fold over and pleat to seal into a half-moon.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high. Sit the gyoza flat-side down and fry until the bottoms are golden, about 2 minutes.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Pour in the water and immediately cover. Steam for <strong>4&ndash;5 minutes</strong> until the water has evaporated and the wrappers are translucent.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Uncover and cook another minute to re-crisp the bases. Serve with a soy and rice vinegar dipping sauce.</p>
</div>
