<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Preheat the oven to <strong>180°C</strong>. Wash the baby potatoes, remove any nasty bits, and halve each one. Cut larger potatoes into two or three pieces so everything is roughly the same size.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Arrange in a single layer in an oven-proof dish — next to each other, <strong>not on top of each other</strong> (any narcissistic potato lying on another should be removed at once; it prolongs the baking time).</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Sprinkle over the oil, garlic, herbs, salt and pepper. Cover the dish with a plate or lid and shake with rolling movements. Check the potatoes are coated evenly; if not, cover and shake again.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bake <strong>uncovered</strong> at 180°C for about <strong>60 minutes</strong>, turning twice — at 20 and 40 minutes — until soft inside and crispy outside. If not crispy enough, give them another 10&ndash;20 minutes.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Variations:</strong> add sausage pieces, mushrooms or peppers after about 20 minutes (use a larger dish so the potatoes stay uncovered). Or bake cubed feta with baby rosa tomatoes and a little oil in a separate covered dish for ~30 minutes, then grill for 3&ndash;5 minutes and stir through with fresh basil.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Andrew's favourite:</strong> add extra salt, pour 125&ndash;250 ml cream over the potatoes 5&ndash;10 minutes before they're done, toss and return to the oven. Drizzle with balsamic reduction when they come out. SERIOUSLY addictive. Serve with dips, sour cream, lemon butter or peppadews.</p>
</div>
