ingredients:
  - name: Honey

    amount: 1

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Make the dough.</strong> In a medium saucepan over medium-low heat, melt the sugar, honey and butter together, stirring occasionally, until smooth (5&ndash;7 minutes).</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Off the heat, slowly pour in the beaten eggs while whisking continuously to prevent scrambling. Whisk in the baking soda, then gradually fold in the flour about ½ cup at a time until a soft dough forms that no longer sticks to your hands.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Bake the layers.</strong> Divide the dough into 8 equal portions. On a well-floured surface, roll each into a thin 9-inch circle, using a plate as a guide to trim neat circles — keep the scraps. Bake each on parchment at <strong>180°C for 4&ndash;5 minutes</strong>, until lightly golden, and cool completely on a wire rack.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bake the leftover scraps until crisp, then crush them into fine crumbs for decorating.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Make the frosting.</strong> Whip the cream to stiff peaks. In a separate bowl, whisk the sour cream and icing sugar, then gently fold in the whipped cream. Refrigerate until needed.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Assemble.</strong> Spread about ⅓ cup of frosting between each layer, pressing gently as you stack. Frost the top and sides, cover with the crushed crumbs, and refrigerate <strong>overnight</strong> so the layers soften before serving.</p>
</div>
