---
layout: recipe
title: Korean Chicken Bao
image: /assets/images/korean-chicken-bao.jpg
description: Soft steamed bao buns stuffed with sticky gochujang-glazed fried chicken.
category: Mains
time: 45 min
servings: 4
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Bao buns (store-bought or steamed)
    amount: 8
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Chicken thighs, boneless
    amount: 500
    unit: "g"
  - name: Cornflour
    amount: 4
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Gochujang
    amount: 2
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Soy sauce
    amount: 2
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Honey
    amount: 2
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Rice vinegar
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Garlic, minced
    amount: 2
    unit: "cloves"
  - name: Sesame oil
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Oil for frying
    amount: 0
    unit: "as needed"
  - name: Cucumber, sliced
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Spring onion & sesame seeds
    amount: 0
    unit: "to garnish"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and toss in the cornflour until coated.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Shallow-fry the chicken in hot oil until golden and cooked through, then drain.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Make the glaze: simmer the gochujang, soy, honey, rice vinegar, garlic and sesame oil in a pan until glossy.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Toss the fried chicken in the glaze to coat.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Steam the bao buns until soft and puffy, about <strong>6&ndash;8 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Fill each bun with chicken and sliced cucumber, then garnish with spring onion and sesame seeds.</p>
</div>
