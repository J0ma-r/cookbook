---
layout: recipe
title: Werner's Lemon Meringue Tart
image: /assets/images/lemon-meringue.svg
description: Classic South African lemon meringue — buttery Marie biscuit crust, tangy condensed-milk lemon filling, pillowy meringue.
category: Desserts
time: 1 hr 5 min
servings: 8
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Marie biscuits, finely crushed
    amount: 1
    unit: "packet"
  - name: Margarine, melted
    amount: 110
    unit: "g"
  - name: Condensed milk
    amount: 1
    unit: "tin"
  - name: Extra-large eggs, separated
    amount: 3
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Lemon juice (up to 100 ml for extra tang)
    amount: 60
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Caster sugar
    amount: 110
    unit: "ml"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mix the finely crushed Marie biscuits with the melted margarine and press evenly into a tart dish.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Whisk the egg yolks, then add the condensed milk and lemon juice. Mix well and pour into the crust.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Whisk the egg whites until just stiff. Gradually add the caster sugar while whisking, until firm peaks form.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Spoon the meringue on top of the filling and spread to cover.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Bake in a preheated oven at <strong>180°C for about 20 minutes</strong>. Switch off the oven, open the door slightly, and leave the tart inside for about <strong>30 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Allow to cool completely before serving.</p>
</div>
