---
layout: recipe
title: Neapolitan Pizza
image: /assets/images/neapolitan-pizza.jpg
description: A long-rise dough for proper Neapolitan-style pizza.
category: Mains
vegetarian: true
time: overnight
servings: 6
difficulty: 3
ingredients:
  - name: Warm water
    amount: 600
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Flour
    amount: 1000
    unit: "g"
  - name: Salt
    amount: 5
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Yeast
    amount: 2
    unit: "g"
  - name: Fresh mozzarella
    amount: 0
    unit: "to top"
  - name: Fresh basil
    amount: 0
    unit: "to top"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mix the water and salt in a bowl.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add 100g of the flour, then the yeast, then the rest of the flour.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mix well and rest for <strong>2 hours</strong>, then divide into balls and rest overnight.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Flatten a ball, add sauce, basil and salt, and bake at <strong>220&deg;C for 10 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add fresh mozzarella and bake <strong>2 minutes more</strong>.</p>
</div>
