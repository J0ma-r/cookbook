<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Make the dough.</strong> Mix the flour, salt and cold water in a stand mixer with a dough hook until a soft, pillowy dough pulls away from the bowl (~30 seconds). Pat into a 15 cm square on a floured surface, cover, and rest for 15 minutes.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Laminate.</strong> Roll into a 46 cm square. Spread the left ⅔ with a third of the butter, leaving a 25 mm border. Fold the unbuttered third over, then the buttered left third over that; pat out air bubbles and seal the edges. Turn 90°, repeat with another third of the butter.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">For the final roll, turn 90° and roll out to 46 x 53 cm. Spread the remaining butter over the whole surface, then roll into a tight log, brushing off excess flour as you go. Trim the ends, cut in half, wrap and chill for 2 hours — <strong>preferably overnight</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Make the custard.</strong> Whisk the flour with 60 ml of the milk until smooth. Boil the sugar, cinnamon stick and water to <strong>104°C</strong> without stirring. Meanwhile scald the remaining milk and whisk it into the flour mixture. Remove the cinnamon stick and pour the syrup in a thin stream into the milk mixture, whisking briskly. Add the vanilla, cool a minute, whisk in the yolks, strain and set aside. It will be thin — that's correct.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Shape.</strong> Heat the oven to <strong>290°C</strong> (top-third rack) for at least 30 minutes. Roll a chilled log to 25 mm diameter, cut into 18 mm pieces and place one cut-side down in each mini-muffin well. With wet thumbs, press the dough flat against the bottom (~1.5 mm) and up the sides to a lip 3 mm above the pan.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Bake.</strong> Fill each cup ¾ full with cool custard and bake until the edges are frilled and brown — <strong>8&ndash;9 minutes</strong> for mini-muffin tins, 15&ndash;17 for classic tins. Cool until just warm, dust generously with confectioners' sugar and cinnamon, and eat the same day.</p>
</div>
