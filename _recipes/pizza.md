---
layout: recipe
title: Pizza Dough
image: /assets/images/pizza.jpg
description: A simple everyday pizza dough.
category: Dough
time: 2 hr 30 min
servings: 2
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Flour
    amount: 500
    unit: "g"
  - name: Salt
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Yeast
    amount: 7
    unit: "g"
  - name: Warm water
    amount: 325
    unit: "ml"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Dissolve the yeast in the warm water.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Combine everything and knead into a smooth dough.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Cover with a damp cloth and rest for <strong>2 hours</strong>.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Flatten, add toppings, and bake at <strong>220&deg;C for 15 minutes</strong>.</p>
</div>
