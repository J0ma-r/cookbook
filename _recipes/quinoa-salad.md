category: Salad
time: 25 min
servings: 8
difficulty: 1
ingredients:
  - name: Quinoa
    amount: 1
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Lemon, zested and juiced
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Dried oregano
    amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Kosher salt
    amount: 0.75
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Black pepper
    amount: 0.25
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: Extra virgin olive oil
    amount: 0.25
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Scallions, sliced
    amount: 4
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Bell peppers, chopped
    amount: 2
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Pitted kalamata olives, roughly chopped
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "cup"
  - name: English cucumber, chopped
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Crumbled feta cheese
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "cup"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Rinse the quinoa well under cold running water (this removes the bitter saponin coating), then cook according to package instructions.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">While the quinoa cooks, make the dressing: add the lemon juice and zest to a large serving bowl with the oregano, salt and pepper. Whisk in the olive oil, then taste and adjust seasoning.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the quinoa <strong>warm</strong> to the bowl with the dressing. Stir to coat and let it cool completely so the flavours soak in.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">While the quinoa cools, chop the vegetables and add them to the bowl as you go: scallions, bell peppers, kalamata olives and cucumber. Stir to combine.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Finish with crumbled feta and serve cold or at room temperature. Optional: garnish with fresh oregano leaves.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Variations:</strong> add chickpeas, grilled chicken or tuna for protein; swap oregano for basil; skip the feta to make it vegan; or swap quinoa for bulgur, barley or lentils.</p>
</div>
