description: Soft, vivid red sponge with a hint of cocoa, layered with tangy cream cheese frosting and glossy chocolate ganache.
category: Desserts
time: 55 min
servings: 12
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Eggs
    amount: 2
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Oil
    amount: 250
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Buttermilk
    amount: 250
    unit: "ml"
  - name: White vinegar
    amount: 15
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Vanilla essence
    amount: 5
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Red food colouring
    amount: 15
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Cake flour
    amount: 375
    unit: "g"
  - name: Sugar
    amount: 420
    unit: "g"
  - name: Cocoa
    amount: 30
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Bicarbonate of soda
    amount: 5
    unit: "ml"
  - name: Salt
    amount: 5
    unit: "ml"
  - name: "Frosting: cream cheese"
    amount: 250
    unit: "g"
  - name: "Frosting: icing sugar"
    amount: 90
    unit: "g"
  - name: "Frosting: vanilla essence"
    amount: 10
    unit: "ml"
  - name: "Ganache: chocolate, chopped"
    amount: 200
    unit: "g"
  - name: "Ganache: cream"
    amount: 160
    unit: "ml"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Grease the cake pans. Beat the eggs lightly, then add the oil, buttermilk, vinegar, vanilla and red food colouring. Mix to combine.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Sift the cake flour, sugar, cocoa, bicarbonate of soda and salt together in a separate bowl. Make a well in the centre, pour in the egg mixture and mix until smooth.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Pour into the prepared pans and bake at <strong>180°C for 20&ndash;30 minutes</strong>, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool completely before frosting.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Frosting:</strong> beat the cream cheese until smooth, then add the icing sugar and vanilla and beat until light and spreadable.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Ganache:</strong> heat the cream until just below boiling and pour over the chopped chocolate. Sit for 2 minutes, then stir until smooth and glossy. Cool slightly until pourable but not runny.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Sandwich the cooled layers with cream cheese frosting and cover the top and sides. Drizzle or pour the ganache over the top and let it set briefly before slicing.</p>
</div>
