description: Flame-grilled, herb-infused ribeye rested on a bed of garlic, thyme and rosemary with salted butter. Ideal medium-rare.
category: Beef
time: 6 hr rest + 20 min
servings: 2
difficulty: 2
ingredients:
  - name: Ribeyes (28-day matured, at least 3 cm thick with a good fat eye)
    amount: 2
    unit: "x 500 g"
  - name: Large lemon
    amount: 1
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Worcestershire sauce
    amount: 0
    unit: "to coat"
  - name: Funky Ouma Braai Salt
    amount: 0
    unit: "to coat"
  - name: Olive oil
    amount: 0
    unit: "to rub"
  - name: Fresh thyme
    amount: 0
    unit: "generous"
  - name: Fresh rosemary
    amount: 0
    unit: "generous"
  - name: Fresh garlic cloves
    amount: 0
    unit: "generous"
  - name: Salted butter
    amount: 2
    unit: "knobs"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Prepare the ribeyes.</strong> Take the steaks out of the fridge at least <strong>6 hours</strong> before cooking. Squeeze the lemon juice over both sides, coat generously with Worcestershire sauce, then cover both sides with Funky Ouma Braai Salt. Rest at room temperature in the marinade for 6 hours.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Prepare the fire.</strong> Use a gas grill on the hottest setting (or a coal fire). The grill should be really hot with some flames still visible — nothing beats flame-grilled ribeye.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Prepare the resting bed.</strong> While the fire heats, roughly chop a generous quantity of garlic, thyme and rosemary — broken enough to release their flavours.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Grill.</strong> Rub the steaks with olive oil (dripping oil creates flames for a great sear). Aim for medium rare — pull at <strong>47°C internal</strong>. Without a thermometer: 2 minutes per side, then another 1.5 minutes per side on a really hot grill.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Rest.</strong> Spread the chopped herbs on a wooden board and lay the steaks straight from the grill on top. Place a knob of butter on each with a stalk of rosemary and thyme, and rest for at least <strong>8 minutes</strong> — they'll come up to perfect temperature while soaking in the herbs.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Remove the herbs and serve on a heated plate.</p>
</div>
