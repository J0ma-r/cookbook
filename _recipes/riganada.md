<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Prepare the bread.</strong> Slice the bread into thick slices. If using stale bread, lightly moisten with water and let the excess drain; if using fresh bread, toast the slices until golden and crisp.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Arrange the bread on a serving platter and drizzle generously with olive oil.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Spoon the chopped or grated tomatoes over each slice and sprinkle lightly with salt if desired.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Top with crumbled feta, then finish with oregano and an extra drizzle of olive oil.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Serve immediately while the bread is still slightly crisp — perfect as a starter, light lunch or meze platter addition.</p>
</div>
