ingredients:
  - name: Prawns, peeled and deveined
    amount: 250

    amount: 150
    unit: "g"
  - name: Calamari tubes or squid heads
    amount: 150
    unit: "g"
  - name: Olive oil
    amount: 2
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Butter
    amount: 2
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Garlic cloves, crushed
    amount: 4
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Chilli flakes (optional)
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: White wine
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "cup"
  - name: Lemon juice
    amount: 2

  - name: Cream
    amount: 1

    amount: 0.5
    unit: "cup"

  - name: Salt & pepper
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Reserved pasta water
    amount: 1
    unit: "cup"

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Cook the seafood.</strong> Heat a splash of olive oil in a large pan. Season the seafood with salt and pepper, then cook in batches until golden brown — prawns need no more than <strong>1 minute per side</strong>; calamari and mussels only a few minutes. Rather undercook slightly, as everything finishes in the sauce. Remove and set aside.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Make the sauce.</strong> In the same pan, melt the butter and cook the garlic and chilli flakes until fragrant.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Pour in the lemon juice and white wine. Bring to a simmer and reduce by half.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Pour in the cream and simmer for about <strong>5 minutes</strong>, until the sauce coats the back of a spoon.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Return the seafood (with any resting juices) and the parsley to the pan and stir to combine.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Toss the sauce with the cooked pasta and a splash of reserved cooking water until glossy and coated. Serve immediately, sprinkled with extra parsley.</p>
</div>
