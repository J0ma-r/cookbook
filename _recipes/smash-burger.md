---
layout: recipe
title: Smash Burger
image: /assets/images/smash-burger.jpg
description: Thin, crispy-edged beef patties smashed on a hot pan with melted cheese.
category: Mains
featured: true
time: 20 min
servings: 2
difficulty: 1
ingredients:
  - name: Beef mince (20% fat)
    amount: 300
    unit: "g"
  - name: Burger buns
    amount: 2
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Cheese slices
    amount: 2
    unit: "pieces"
  - name: Onion, thinly sliced
    amount: 0.5
    unit: "piece"
  - name: Salt & pepper
    amount: 0
    unit: "to taste"
  - name: Butter (for buns)
    amount: 1
    unit: "tbsp"
  - name: Burger sauce or ketchup & mustard
    amount: 0
    unit: "to serve"
  - name: Pickles & lettuce
    amount: 0
    unit: "optional"
---

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Divide the mince into loose balls without compacting them. Don't season yet.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Get a heavy pan or griddle screaming hot. Toast the buttered buns cut-side down, then set aside.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Place a ball on the dry hot pan and <strong>smash it flat</strong> with a spatula for 10 seconds. Season the top with salt and pepper.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Cook for about 2 minutes until the edges are crisp and browned, then flip.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add a cheese slice and cook another minute until melted. Press the sliced onion into the patty as it cooks if you like.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text">Stack onto the toasted bun with sauce and any extras. Serve immediately.</p>
</div>
