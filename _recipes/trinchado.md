<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Build the base.</strong> Fry the onion, garlic and chilli in olive oil until softened.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the paprika, cayenne pepper, salt, lemon &amp; pepper spice and tomato paste. Stir to combine.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the Worcestershire sauce, red wine, vinegar, lemon juice and water. Simmer for a few minutes, then stir in the cream.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Add the beef, stir, and simmer on low/medium heat for <strong>2&ndash;3 hours</strong> (the longer, the better), stirring intermittently.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text">Serve with fresh bread and butter to mop up the sauce. <strong>Tip:</strong> it tastes best made a few days ahead — the flavours deepen beautifully.</p>
</div>
