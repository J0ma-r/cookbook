<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <p class="step-text">Preheat the oven to <strong>177°C (350°F)</strong>. Grease two 9-inch round cake pans, line with parchment rounds, and grease the parchment.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <p class="step-text">Whisk the flour, cocoa, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, salt and espresso powder together in a large bowl. In another bowl, mix the oil, eggs and vanilla on medium-high until combined, then mix in the buttermilk.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <p class="step-text">Pour the dry ingredients into the wet, add the hot coffee, and mix on low until completely combined. <strong>The batter will be thin — that's correct.</strong></p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">4</div>
  <p class="step-text">Divide between the pans and bake <strong>23&ndash;26 minutes</strong>, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool in the pans for 1 hour, then turn out onto a rack to finish cooling.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">5</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Buttercream:</strong> beat the butter until creamy (~2 minutes). Add the confectioners' sugar, cocoa, 3 tbsp cream, vanilla and salt; beat on low for 30 seconds, then on high for 1 full minute. Adjust with more sugar/cocoa if too thin, or more cream if too thick.</p>
</div>
<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">6</div>
  <p class="step-text"><strong>Assemble.</strong> Level the cooled layers if domed. Frost the first layer, top with the second, and cover the top and sides with the remaining frosting. Garnish with chocolate chips and refrigerate 30&ndash;60 minutes before slicing. Store covered in the fridge up to 5 days.</p>
</div>
